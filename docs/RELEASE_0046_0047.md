# Release notes — migrations 0046 & 0047 (Oct 2026)

## 0046 · View-only share links

**Why:** an organisation wants its results in front of 90+ field leaders without
giving each an account.

**What:** Dashboard → *View-only links* → name, scope (all surveys or one room),
expiry (30 days–1 year), optional passcode, heat map on/off, detail on/off.
The link (`/view/<64 hex>`) is shown once; only its sha256 is stored.

**Holds, by construction**
- Same cores, same minimums as the dashboard. A room below `room_min_n` shows
  its count only; the house usually clears `min_group_n` first.
- No Collab overlay or benchmark on the organisation's matrices.
- Heat map = the dashboard's map: `collab_intelligence()` countries (all data
  per country, the 2,000 gate, the `publish_global_view` switch) + outlines
  where this scope reached its floor.
- Unknown / revoked / expired → one `unavailable` answer. Passcodes lock after
  `share_link_passcode_max_failures` for `share_link_lockout_minutes`.
- Deleting a room deletes its share links (never widens them to the house).
- `/view/*`: noindex, no-referrer, no-store; never in the service-worker cache.

**Also changed:** the dashboard trend drops years below `min_group_n`
followers (it previously showed a year however few people it held).

## 0047 · Non-followers counted in completions, scored apart

- `completions = {total, following, not_following}` on the house, season and
  room cores. *Completed the Index* shows the total with its split. `n` is
  unchanged: the J12's own n.
- The **Unengaged matrix** (Exploration Index) is now a view beside the J12 on
  the dashboard and share links, for the house, a season and — new — a room.
  It unlocks at the same floor the J12 holds in that space.
- Fixed: `blended_trend`, `org_benchmark`, `network_console` and
  `org_dashboard_admin` averaged non-followers' answers into J12 figures. Each
  gets one branch condition; nothing else in their bodies changed.
- `retained_aggregates` gains `branch` (in its key); the purge archives each
  branch apart. No purge has run, so nothing existing is relabelled.
- `org_dashboard_demo` now reads `_org_dashboard_core`.

## Not changed (flagged)
- `collab_intelligence()` places respondents by their *organisation's* country,
  while reach outlines use the respondent's reported country.
- Collab Intelligence's own tab doesn't yet show an Unengaged matrix view.
- Room cards count completed sessions; the house card now counts completions
  with scored answers — they can differ by a respondent who completed without
  a scored answer.
- Pre-existing stale smoke tests: 0036 (retired `public` audience) and 0042
  (retired `facilitator` role).
